Five business reports built in Excel using Power Pivot and DAX measures,
completed as part of the Codebasics Excel course.

## Business questions answered

1. Top 10 products by percentage growth in net sales, 2020 to 2021
2. Division-level net sales with year-over-year growth
3. Top 5 and bottom 5 products by quantity sold
4. New products introduced in 2021
5. Top 5 countries by net sales in 2021

## Approach

- Data model with dimension tables (product, customer, market, date)
  related to a fact table of monthly sales
- DAX measures for net sales by year and growth percentage
- Value filters for top-N and bottom-N selection
- Conditional formatting to highlight relative performance

## Tools

Excel, Power Pivot, DAX, PivotTables
